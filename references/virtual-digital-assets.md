# Crypto and NFTs — virtual digital assets

A **VDA** is cryptocurrency, NFTs and similar assets. They are taxed under a
regime of their own, harsher than capital gains, and the harshness is the point to
convey early.

## s.115BBH — flat 30%

Gains on transfer of a VDA are taxed at a **flat 30%**, plus cess, in **both**
regimes. No slab. No s.87A rebate.

**Only the cost of acquisition is deductible.** Not exchange fees, not gas, not
infrastructure, not interest.

**Losses do not set off — against anything.** Not against other VDA gains, not
against other heads, and they do not carry forward. Each gaining transfer is taxed
on its own, so a year that nets to a loss overall can still owe tax.

That asymmetry is the single most surprising rule for most taxpayers. Say it
plainly before they see the number.

## s.194S — 1% TDS

The transferee deducts **1%** on the consideration. Indian exchanges do it
automatically, so those transfers appear in **26AS** — a useful cross-check on
whether the taxpayer's trade list is complete.

Peer-to-peer and foreign-exchange transfers often carry no TDS, which changes
nothing about the tax owed. Missing TDS is not missing liability.

## Schedule VDA

**Every transfer is a row.** The schedule is transaction-wise, not a summary:
date of acquisition, date of transfer, head under which taxed, cost of
acquisition, consideration, and income.

The column total carries only **positive** incomes — a direct consequence of losses
not setting off.

Where an exchange statement runs to hundreds of transfers, the schedule still wants
each one. Check for a CSV upload on the schedule before entering rows by hand.

## Reading an exchange statement

Aim for one row per **transfer**, which includes more than a sale for INR:

- crypto sold for INR
- **crypto swapped for another crypto** — a transfer of the first asset, taxable
  even though no rupees moved
- crypto spent on goods or services
- NFT sales

Airdrops, mining and staking rewards are taxed on receipt as income, then again on
eventual transfer with that receipt value as cost.

A holding merely held, or moved between the taxpayer's own wallets, is not a
transfer and does not appear.

## What flows where

Schedule VDA total → Schedule CG item C2 (income from transfer of VDAs) → the
capital-gains line in Part B-TI → the 30% row in Schedule SI.

Verify at Schedule SI that the VDA tax equals total positive VDA income × 30%, and
that no loss has quietly reduced it.
