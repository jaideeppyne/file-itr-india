# Old-regime deductions

These are what can make the old regime cheaper, so collect the proofs and total
them before running the comparison. Re-confirm the current year's limits.

Count only what the taxpayer actually holds and can prove. An old-regime figure
inflated with aspirational investments produces a comparison that recommends the
wrong regime.

> Two survive in the **new** regime as well: the employer's NPS contribution under
> **80CCD(2)**, and the standard deduction. Everything else below is old-regime only.

## The big ones

| Deduction | Limit | Proof to collect |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard deduction** (salary) | ₹50,000 | automatic |
| **80C / 80CCC / 80CCD(1)** combined | ₹1,50,000 | EPF statement, PPF passbook, ELSS, life-insurance premium receipts, home-loan principal certificate, children's tuition receipts, 5-yr tax-saver FD, NSC, Sukanya Samriddhi |
| **80CCD(1B)** — NPS | extra ₹50,000, over and above 80C | NPS statement |
| **80CCD(2)** — employer NPS | up to 10% of salary (14% govt) | Form 16 Part B — **works in both regimes** |
| **80D** — health insurance | ₹25,000 self+family (₹50,000 if senior); **plus** ₹25,000 parents (₹50,000 if senior); preventive check-up ₹5,000 within these | premium receipts |
| **Home-loan interest** (s.24b) | ₹2,00,000 self-occupied; uncapped let-out, set-off against other heads limited to ₹2L | lender's interest certificate |
| **HRA** (s.10(13A)) | least of: actual HRA / rent − 10% salary / 50% salary metro, 40% non-metro | rent receipts, landlord PAN if rent > ₹1L a year, HRA line from Form 16 |

## Worth checking

- **80TTA** — savings-bank interest to ₹10,000. **80TTB** — ₹50,000 of interest for
  senior citizens.
- **80E** — education-loan interest, uncapped, for up to 8 years.
- **80G** — donations, 50% or 100% deductible depending on the institution, some
  subject to a 10%-of-income qualifying limit. Cash donations above ₹2,000 do not
  qualify. Needs the donee's PAN and an 80G receipt.
- **80EEB** — electric-vehicle loan interest, to ₹1,50,000.
- **80DD / 80DDB / 80U** — disability and specified-illness deductions.
- **80GG** — rent paid where no HRA is received.
- **LTA** (s.10(5)) — domestic travel, twice in a block of four years.

## Carried-forward losses

Not a deduction, but the same question and often forgotten: a loss carried forward
from an earlier year sets off against this year's gains and can wipe out the tax
entirely.

The portal pre-fills Schedule CFL from prior returns, so the fastest check is to
open Schedule CFL and read it — an all-zero table settles the question in seconds.
Where the taxpayer is unsure, that beats asking them.

## Feeding the comparison

Old-regime taxable income = gross income − ₹50,000 standard deduction − 80C (to
₹1.5L) − 80CCD(1B) (to ₹50k) − 80D − home-loan interest − HRA exemption − the rest,
floored at zero. Apply the old-regime slabs, add cess, and compare against the
new-regime total.

Where the taxpayer is unsure what they hold, list the categories and ask rather
than assuming zero — an unasked 80C is the most common reason someone overpays.
